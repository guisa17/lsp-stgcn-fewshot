## LSA64 encoders

The ST-GCN and HA-GCN encoders listed in `MODEL_CARD.md` are meta-trained on LSA64, which is
distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International
License (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Because the dataset's terms ask derivative works to keep the same
license, these weights will be released under **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** as well:

- **Attribution.** Credit this repository and the LSA64 dataset (see below).
- **NonCommercial.** Commercial use is not permitted.
- **ShareAlike.** Models derived from these weights must use the same license.

License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

Required attribution for LSA64 (dataset page: https://facundoq.github.io/datasets/lsa64/):

> F. Ronchetti, F. Quiroga, C. Estrebou, L. Lanzarini and A. Rosete. "LSA64: An Argentinian Sign
> Language Dataset." XXII Congreso Argentino de Ciencias de la Computación (CACIC), 2016.

## Models trained on LSP recordings

Models trained on the LSP recordings of this project are not released, because the recordings are
personal data and not every signer has signed a consent form for publication.
